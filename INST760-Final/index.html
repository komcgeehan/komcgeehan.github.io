How is social media use associated with persistent sadness or hopelessness among U.S. high school students? These views explore the overall pattern and how that relationship differs by sex.

How to Read These Findings

These results show associations, not causation. The YRBS is a cross-sectional, self-reported survey, so the visualization does not suggest that social media use causes sadness or hopelessness. Percentages shown here are weighted estimates from the 2023 National YRBS.

Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, 2023 National Youth Risk Behavior Survey.
