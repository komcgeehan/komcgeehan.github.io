Brief

A monthly newsletter to engage donors, lapsed donors, sustainers, and Circle of Stars members across four audience versions during World Wish Month.

Approach

Led with a wish kid story to anchor the emotional core, then layered in partner integrations and giving opportunities without losing the human thread.

Result

Sent across four audience versions — donors, lapsed donors, sustainers, and Circle of Stars members — with content blocks tailored to each relationship tier while maintaining a single cohesive editorial voice.
