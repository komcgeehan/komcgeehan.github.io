The countdown timer makes urgency visible
Menstrual Health Day is Plan International USA's highest-revenue campaign moment of the year. The midnight deadline isn't decorative — it's the engine of the appeal, and the timer puts that pressure on the page in real time.
The gift chart makes the match tangible
Showing donors exactly what their gift becomes removes the abstraction from matching gift copy. "$25 → $75" is more motivating than "your gift will be tripled" — it lets donors see their own impact before they give.
The P.S. functions as a second ask
During Plan's biggest fundraising campaign, every touchpoint matters. The P.S. recaps the deadline and the match in one sentence — capturing donors who skimmed the body copy and scrolled straight to the bottom.

Brief

A day-of countdown email for Plan International USA's Menstrual Health Day campaign — the organization's most significant annual fundraising moment — driving last-chance gifts before a midnight match deadline.

Approach

Leaned into the urgency architecture that makes MHD such a strong revenue driver: a visible countdown, a concrete match calculator, and a P.S. that recaps both for donors who skim. The copy stays mission-grounded throughout, so urgency never tips into pressure.

Result

Deployed on Menstrual Health Day itself — May 28 — to maximize the impact of the midnight deadline and drive last-chance gifts during Plan International USA's most significant annual fundraising moment.
