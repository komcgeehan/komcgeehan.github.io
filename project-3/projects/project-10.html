The MMS

Holiday GOH · MMS

Reaching donors where they are — with the catalog in hand

The GOH #2 email targets past Gifts of Hope purchasers — donors who already understand the catalog model and just need a timely reason to return. The subject line leads with the matched gift offer, not the catalog itself, because the match is what's new this year.

Each catalog item — chickens, sheep, a backpack, menstrual health kits — is presented in a single sentence with a photo, keeping the copy scannable and letting the images carry the emotional weight. The CTA arrives only after the donor has browsed the options.

↻ The catalog image animates as a GIF, cycling through gift options — chickens or ducks, sheep, a backpack, and menstrual health kits — before the recipient even opens the message.

Characters

Audience

Brief

A November Gifts of Hope email targeting past GOH purchasers in the last two years, paired with a holiday MMS sent to all opted-in phone numbers on file.

Approach

Treated returning GOH purchasers as a warm audience who already value the catalog format — leading with the match offer and letting the familiar gift items do the persuasion work.

Result

Part of a multi-touch holiday campaign that included three GOH emails, a Good Giving Guide, a homepage hero, and the MMS — each channel reinforcing the matched gift message across the full donor journey.
