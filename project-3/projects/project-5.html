Hero headline does double duty
The "3X MATCH" framing leads with the offer, but the subhead pivots immediately to impact — grounding the math in a girl's future rather than a transaction.
Anna's story makes the mission specific
Rather than describing girls in the abstract, the copy anchors in one girl's dream of becoming a doctor — making the ask feel personal and proximate.
The sign-off earns its sentiment
"From the very beginning, girls' dreams begin with you" closes the loop on the opening theme — linking the donor's gift back to the earliest moment of a child's life.

Brief

A year-end appeal email for Plan International USA, written in three audience versions, asking lapsed and active donors to make a matched gift before December 31.

Approach

Centered the copy on a single child — Anna, a preschooler in Laos — to make the global mission feel immediate. The 3X match framing led with impact rather than mechanics, keeping the emotional thread intact across all three versions.
