Emergency Appeal · March 2025

The campaign

Homepage Hero

Entry point for new visitors during a live emergency

The homepage hero replaced Plan's standard banner within hours of the conflict escalating — giving every site visitor an immediate, actionable way to respond to the crisis.

Desktop Lightbox

Interrupts existing traffic to surface the emergency appeal

The lightbox intercepted donors already on the site — people with existing relationships with Plan — and redirected their attention to the most urgent need. The warning icon and all-caps "URGENT" signal danger before the donor reads a single word.

Social Ad

Extends reach beyond the donor file in real time

Paid social allowed Plan to reach prospective donors outside its existing email list. The photo grounds the emergency in a specific human moment — a child, a caregiver, an act of care — before the red bar escalates to urgency.

Display Ad

Reinforces the campaign message across the web

Display ads followed prospective donors across the web, keeping the emergency visible beyond a single visit. The consistent "Rush life-saving aid" headline threads through every channel — creating a unified campaign voice at scale.

Brief

A rapid response campaign launched within days of escalating Middle East conflict, requiring copy across multiple channels simultaneously — homepage, lightbox, social, display, email, and SMS.

Approach

Maintained a consistent urgent voice across every touchpoint, with copy calibrated to each channel's constraints — from a 340-character SMS to a full homepage hero — while keeping the human cost of conflict at the center of every ask.
